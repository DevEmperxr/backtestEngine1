# Data split for strategy development

*(Agreed with the user on 2026-10-06, from run 026 onward. Earlier runs, 001–025, used their own
pre-registered years and are not affected.)*

| Role | Years | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Practice (development)** | **2023, 2024** | Try ideas and improvements here. Keep a change only if it helps in **both** practice years. |
| **Test (confirmation)** | **2021, 2022, 2025** | Touched only once per finished, pre-registered version. No tuning after looking. A result here is reported as is. |
| **Emergency holdout** | **2026** | Not downloaded, not loaded, not tested, unless the user explicitly releases it. |

Why: by run 025, Idea 1 had been varied six times on 2023 alone. Continuing to tune on one year
would eventually produce a lucky "winner" (as happened with 2024 in runs 001–015). Two practice
years filter out improvements that only fit one year's noise. The test years stay clean for an
honest final check.

Notes:
- 2024 was the development year for runs 001–015 (other strategies). It is still unseen for the
  sweep-fade (Idea 1) and turn-confirmation (Idea 2) programmes.
- 2023 has been looked at for Ideas 1 and 2 (runs 022–025); it remains a practice year.
- Each finished version still gets a pre-registration (hypothesis, rules, pass bar) before its
  test-year run, as in `strategy_research_protocol.md`.
